## Selected Portfolio Work

### Assignments

The assignments introduced the professional responsibilities of data curators and examined how organizations treat data as a strategic asset. They also explored the FAIR principles and their role in making data findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable.

### Labs

The labs provided hands-on experience with dataset discovery, quality evaluation, data documentation, unstructured data, data annotation, synthetic data, governance, privacy, and career development.

### Midterm Project

The midterm project focused on developing a data-curation plan and pilot. It required the selection and evaluation of a dataset, identification of data-quality concerns, documentation of the proposed curation process, and consideration of responsible data practices.

### Final Project

The final project brought together the major concepts covered throughout the course. It demonstrated the ability to evaluate a dataset, identify quality issues, document the cleaning and preparation process, communicate technical findings, and present the data as a reliable and AI-ready resource.

## Professional Reflection

Completing this portfolio strengthened my understanding that successful artificial-intelligence systems depend on more than algorithms. They require data that is accurate, relevant, representative, properly documented, legally usable, secure, and responsibly managed.

The course also showed me that data curation is an ongoing process. Data must be reviewed, updated, validated, protected, and documented throughout its lifecycle. These skills are valuable for careers in artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, data engineering, robotics, and responsible AI.
